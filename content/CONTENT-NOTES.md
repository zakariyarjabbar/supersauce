# Content and source notes

Prepared for the user-authorized mock website on 5 September 2026.

## What is supplied

The user supplied the Super Sauce logo, local reference screenshots and the statement that the restaurant has more than 23 branches throughout Iraq. The user explicitly approved invented/sample content until the restaurant accepts the proposed website.

The reference screenshots establish the red identity, yellow star, large Arabic campaign typography and checkerboard details. One unrelated Discord screenshot in the reference folder is not a design reference and was not treated as instructions.

## Public references

- [Super Sauce Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/supersauce.iq/) — public profile identified in the user's brand screenshots.
- [Talabat Iraq — Super Sauce](https://www.talabat.com/ar/iraq/super-sauce) — menu naming reference and public ordering destination.
- [Baly — Super Sauce](https://food.baly.iq/vendors/super-sauce/) — public ordering destination.

The project uses these as references and outbound links, not a live feed. Public listing availability and pricing can change. No personal social account, private message or customer information was accessed.

## Presentation content

Menu descriptions, listed ingredients, prices, meal compositions, hours, sample branch addresses, delivery fee and corporate copy are illustrative. Some menu names and branch areas are informed by public references; this is not a verified current operational dataset. The complete list needs restaurant approval before publication as an official site.

The eight sample branch records do not purport to enumerate all 23+ locations. The restaurant exterior image is an architectural concept. No actual job vacancy, award, founding date, customer rating or testimonial was invented.

Seven food/architecture images were generated specifically for the website. They are distinct from actual product photography. The exact prompts and provenance are recorded alongside the source assets and in `asset-prompts.json`.

Default demo mode keeps the presentation out of search engines and explains the mock behavior in the footer, checkout, form feedback, FAQ and legal pages.
